About Me


I specialize in engineering high-impact digital solutions where business strategy meets technical excellence. With an MBA and a background in management, I bring a rare, macro-level perspective to software development—ensuring every line of code serves a functional, real-world purpose.

As a Full-Stack Software Engineer, I have successfully architected and deployed complex systems, ranging from automated school portals and e-commerce engines to secure social media platforms. My technical toolkit includes React, Node.js, PHP, and Firebase, complemented by professional certifications in Cybersecurity and JavaScript from Google and IBM.

I thrive in fast-paced, remote environments where I can manage the full project lifecycle. Whether I am optimizing database schemas or crafting intuitive user interfaces, my goal is to deliver robust, scalable software that solves complex problems with surgical precision.
